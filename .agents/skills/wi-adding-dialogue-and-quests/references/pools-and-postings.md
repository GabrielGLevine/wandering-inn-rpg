# Pools, stages, boards, and postings

Conversation/talk pool stages are ordered permanent registers with later-match
selection. Trace every reachable player state and identify the winning stage
before appending. One-shot acknowledgement flavor may yield to a warm terminal;
guidance and unlock hints must remain reachable, often by positioning above the
terminal or repeating the line in the warm/neutral pool. Permanent standing
stages belong above one-shot acknowledgements. Once any stage arms, base-pool
appends may be unreachable. Ambient barks must fit their current copy-fit limit.

Boolean board/desk gates hide in both directions; prove mutually exclusive rows
with exact visible-option arrays in both states. Do not use `hide_when` on a
monotone accepted/completed counter unless permanent retirement is intended.
Slot exclusivity should prevent double acceptance without permanently hiding a
re-offer after abandonment.

A desk settles only its own postings. Inspect the current bounty ownership
resolver rather than using display prose such as `giver` as a machine key. A
foreign turn-in/abandon must render feedback without consuming the posting, and
tests cover each voice, completion, and foreign/local combination. Signatures
belong in authored copy in the character’s voice.

Adding a visible hub row shifts cursor-driven QA even when no exact options array
is pinned. Search every route using that conversation and evaluate its fixture
counters. Adding leads changes journal lines for every matching fixture; derive
the new rendered pins from real runs. When lambdas observe mutable test state,
mutate the captured object rather than rebinding a local that was captured by
value.
