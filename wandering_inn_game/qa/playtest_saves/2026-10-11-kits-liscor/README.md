## Where it puts you

`street` at cell **[12,4]**, facing north at the market row (bread stall, Krshia's stall and the bread oven against the city wall), at **dusk** (`actions_since_sleep` 450). Post-game warrior 10; no encounter in reach.

## What to do

1. Look along the market row, the tan brick wall behind it and the gate to the west.
2. Walk south past the kerb to the Watch-fire plaza (campfire, lantern, sewer manhole) and on to the wood yard and the stall counter in the southeast.
3. Enter the Watch Barracks [22,2], the Adventurer's Guild [28,2] and the Runner's Guild [20,16].
4. Do not sleep: sleeping resets the clock to day.

## What to judge

- Does the street read as warm sandstone brick, plaster and clay roofs, distinct from Invrisil and the Floodplains? Do the wall and the paving separate?
- Does anything on the street repeat visibly or crowd a walking lane?
- Barracks: do the brick walls, kit rack (peg rail, shield, sword), lockers and duty table read as a Watch room?
- Runners' Guild: do the sorting shelf, desk and ledger read as delivery sorting?
- Adventurer's Guild: do the counter, chalk board and back-wall cabinet read as reception? (Its timber walls and round tables are main's.)
- At dusk, do the two guild windows glow and do the lamps have visible sources?
