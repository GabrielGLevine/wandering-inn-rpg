# Attribution

Art, audio, and other media in this repository and in official builds,
with their licenses and required credits. Community contributions are
added here as they merge (see `CONTRIBUTING-ASSETS.md`).

## Setting

The Wandering Inn is a web serial by **pirateaba**
(https://wanderinginn.com). This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan
project; setting, characters, and lore belong to pirateaba.

## Audio

- **Super Dialogue Audio Pack** by **Dillon Becker** — CC BY 4.0.

## Repository media

Per-pack license notes and verdicts live in
`wandering_inn_game_v4/assets/LICENSES/`. Assets whose licenses do not
permit redistribution are not present in this repository; official builds
add them from a private bundle, and the public build uses fallback art in
their place.
