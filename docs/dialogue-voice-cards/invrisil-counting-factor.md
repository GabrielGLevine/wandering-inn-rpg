## invrisil_counting_factor.json

**BANNED:** Ban 7 (prose triad), `threshold`: "The front takes terms, the side
takes timing, and the rear takes more shoulder than sense." — three balanced
clauses announcing the pocket's three routes in one sentence, which is the
ban's textbook instance, plus a wit closer on a hub node (ban 2). Dies; see
sample. Ban 2, `terms`: "…whatever waits inside is your own arithmetic. Those
are the terms." — arithmetic-as-epigram, the poster line of the file; dies, and
NOT by truncation (§4 forbids amputation — the slot is refilled).

**Watch-list, KEEP:** `rear_account`'s "That rear bar was expensive." stays —
flat, priced, and the only acknowledgement the force route gets. Its
"say what you came to say" stays: a factor ending a subject is in register.
Player option labels stay plain and transactional ("Leave the account closed.",
"Agreed.") — no epigrams in the player's mouth either (bible §7.3).

**FORCED:** The route list is **broken, never enumerated**. He may not count
the ways in. Two of them are things he sells; the third arrives as *reportage*
about damage other people have done ("Men have put a shoulder through the rear
door before now"), which is how a factor refuses to dignify something without
saying so. This is deliberately NOT the shepherd's miscount move
(`riverfarm-shepherd+bark.md`: name two, forget the third, hand it back a turn
later) — that shape is spoken for, and re-using it would build a template out
of ban 7's remedy.

Both rewritten lines end per §4 **move 1 (put a thing in the line)**: the
ironwood bar in its stone brackets, and the key on his cuff. Both objects are
already his — the bar is `counting_room_rear_bar`'s own copy verbatim in
feeling, and the key is in his `observe` line ("The key at his cuff is meant to
be seen"). No move-3 unfinished-fact endings here: he finishes his sentences,
because finishing them is the service.

**CANON-VOICE:** Game-original. An Invrisil counting-house factor: the man who
holds the door of a room where merchants settle what they would rather not
write down. Menace is entirely commercial — he has never threatened anybody and
has never needed to. He is bored by the front door, mildly professional about
the side shutter, and privately contemptuous of the rear one, and none of that
is ever stated. Spoiler bar: the fence thread the bale exposes belongs to the
hat-and-feather back room; he never names it and never will.

**FACTS, do not lose (three modes are taught here and nowhere else):** front =
terms, set by him ([Bargain]); side shutter = timing, against a watchman on a
fixed round ([Appraise Foe]); rear door = a shoulder against an ironwood bar in
stone brackets ([Greater Strength]). A rewrite may move which sentence carries
which, but all three must survive in this node.

**SAMPLE:** node `threshold` —
> Before: `The room is closed. The front takes terms, the side takes timing, and the rear takes more shoulder than sense.`
> After: `The room is closed. The front door takes terms, and I set the terms myself. The side shutter opens if you can time the watchman's round. Men have put a shoulder through the rear door before now. The bar back there is ironwood in stone brackets.`

The balanced triad becomes an unbalanced four: an offer he is proud of, a
condition he doubts you can meet, a thing that has happened to him, and the
masonry it happened to. Avg 9.0 words/sentence, one subordinate clause. All
three routes still taught.

**SAMPLE:** node `terms` —
> Before: `One look, no hands on the ledgers, and whatever waits inside is your own arithmetic. Those are the terms.`
> After: `One look, and no hands on the ledgers. Whatever is standing in that room when the door opens is yours to deal with. Say the word and the key comes off my cuff.`

"Your own arithmetic" was the file's best line and its worst problem. What
replaces it is the *literal* warning the epigram was decorating — something is
standing in that room — and the contract closes on the key coming off his cuff
rather than on a phrase. Avg 11.0 words/sentence.
