**FORCED:** T1 stats: avg ≤8 words/sentence. Shop nodes end on goods or prices, never on jokes. Add one human wobble: a correction or a hedge ("mostly honest. Mostly.") is in register — self-repeat, not punchline.

## razorbeak_nest.json

**BANNED:** Ban 11, bark template closer: "Alone, they'd already be running. There are two of them." — assessment-verdict wit; dies.

**FORCED:** Shape: **sound-first** — the screech leads, motion follows, no closing judgment. 2–4 sentences. The pair-bond fact (two birds, bolder together, guarding a scrape) must survive as observable behavior.

**CANON-VOICE:** Narrator, nesting Razorbeaks. Aggressive scavenger-birds defending a nest scrape — the scene is noise, wings, and ground they will not give.

**SAMPLE:** node `confront` —
> Before: `Two long-beaked shapes shuffle up from a scrape of trampled grass, wings half-spread, screeching thin and high. Alone, they'd already be running. There are two of them.`
> After: `The screeching starts before you see them, thin and high and doubled. Two long-beaked shapes shuffle up from a scrape of trampled grass, wings half-spread. Neither bird gives ground. Each keeps one eye on the other.`
