# Cluster: invrisil-steward — T3 envoy + T2 rest factor

Read `docs/dialogue-voice-bible.md` first. Two professionals of discretion at different stations: the carriage-woman speaks for a great house (T3), the factor runs a room where nobody asks questions (re-tiered T2 — publican register). Antithesis grants: envoy 1 — **pinned: "My mistress does not do favours. She does accounts." (`answered`)**; factor zero.

## invrisil_house_steward.json

**BANNED:** Ban 1/anaphora (worst-lines table): "I am not waiting for you and I am not waiting for the house. I am waiting for a question to be answered" (`waiting`) — textbook anaphora-then-reveal; dies (see sample). Beyond the pin: "the name was being sold, not taken" (`answered`) — the sold/taken distinction is the quest's *factual answer* and must survive, but unbalanced ("Then he was selling it, and nobody had to take it from him."); "That is a different letter to write" (hub tv[1]) — keep, flat and fine. Ban 2: "and neither of those is a favour." (`answered`) — redundant once the pin lands; fold it in. Watch-list: "I sit somewhere warm and I am extremely dull." stays — professional self-description, and the corpus needs someone content to be dull. "Nobody in this house has ever once looked at what is on the table." stays. "and she settles them the same week." stays — the pin's tail, factual.

**FORCED:** Messenger discipline: she reports, she does not editorialize; emotion appears only as precision about logistics (the card, the hand, the letter). PEAK: `answered` — the pinned antithesis is the file's one button.

**CANON-VOICE:** A woman who came in a carriage — envoy of the unseen house that runs the city (keep it unnamed — spoiler bar). Game-original: unhurried, unimpressable, professionally forgettable and proud of the craft of it. Warmth rationed to one degree above room temperature.

**SAMPLE:** node `waiting` —
> Before: `Sit if you like. I am not waiting for you and I am not waiting for the house. I am waiting for a question to be answered, and I would rather do that in a warm room than in a cold one.`
> After: `Sit if you like. My waiting has nothing to do with you, or with this house. A question went out in her hand. When its answer exists, I will carry it back. Until then there is a fire here, and I intend to use it.`

The anaphora-reveal dies; the same information arrives as itinerary — and the fire becomes a thing she uses rather than a rhetorical pole.

## invrisil_rest_factor.json

(`exchange_one`) — balanced epigram; dies, but KEEP "Which would you like it to have been?" — the best question in the corpus. Watch-list: "he'll be back for it or he won't, and either way the coat is paid for through the season." stays — flat, ominous, perfect. "we have never once had to say it twice." stays.

**FORCED:** T2 re-tier: publican clip — tables, coats, seasons, tabs. House rules delivered as furniture facts (move 1 is his native mode; the room does his threatening for him). PEAK: `exchange_three` — the coat rewrite is the file's landed moment; nothing else closes hard.

**CANON-VOICE:** The factor of the Adventurer's Rest — the room where Invrisil's quiet business drinks. Game-original: innkeeper-adjacent, memory like a ledger, menace entirely outsourced to implication. Nothing surprises him on the premises, by policy.

**SAMPLE:** node `table` —
> Before: `You'll want the long table, {addr}, not this one. Long table's for people waiting on somebody. This one's for people who have already arrived.`
> After: `You'll want the long table, {addr}. That's the waiting table. This one's mine, and the gentleman who shares it didn't wait for anybody. Sit where the evening puts you.`

The mirrored pair becomes seating logistics with one unexplained occupant (move 3) — the room stays stranger than any epigram made it.
