# Dialogue corpus critique — external, 2026-08-03

> External critique received 2026-08-03; rubric for the dialogue voice pass.
> Verbatim below. File references were relative to `wandering_inn_game/data/dialogue/`.

## Percentage

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Written by a model? | ~100%. Uniform hand across all 72 files, including files touched weeks apart. |
| Would a trained reader flag it? | ~85%. |
| Carries a hard single-line tell (measured) | **33%** of nodes — and that undercounts, since ~90 nodes are 1-line shop/combat barks too short to tell. Among nodes >200 chars: closer to **70%**. |
| Reads genuinely idiosyncratic / human | ~5%. Rags' pidgin, the drayman, Pell, Ksmvr's ledger-brain. |

Nothing here reads as human-written-then-edited. Tell density is even, so no seam between "human draft" and "AI fill".

## Characteristic tells (ranked by damage)

**1. Antithesis addiction — "X, not Y."** The signature. 185 instances of the bare pattern; every NPC does it regardless of class, species, literacy.
> "Light is a ratio, not a number." · "That isn't a rule of the house. That's the rent." · "Funding, not evidence." · "Fences before deer." · "Whatever's holding them isn't teeth." · "A map IS as good as a corpse."

Real speech uses this maybe once per scene as a rhetorical peak. Here it *is* the default sentence shape. Watch Captain, tea-fixer, goblin chieftain and a stone-golem barker all reach for it.

**2. Every node lands a button.** Setup → turn → epigram, ~200 times. Humans let lines die flat; models can't resist the closer.
> "It's the tail, nobody expects it." · "Long may it bore us." · "That's me. I'm the furniture." · "Nothing ever works the way anyone hopes." · "The craft does its accounting at rest."

**3. Sentiment-then-deflect template** (13 nodes, near-identical): warm beat, instant defensive quip. Every single "served" node runs it.
> "It is good. Thank you. I will deny the tone I said it in." (Zevara) · "Do not tell anyone I said so." (Selys) · "Now stop making me sincere during business hours." (Selys again) · "stand still while it lands." (Relc) · "Tell no one." (Rags) · "no more feelings before lunch, house rule." (Erin)

One emotional template, fifteen coats of paint.

**4. CAPS-shouting** (44 nodes). `DIDN'T`, `RECOVER`, `FED`, `RUN`, `SEEN`, `MOVE ALONG`, `OH`. Chat-model typography, not script typography. A human game writer uses italics or nothing — and wouldn't apply it identically in 30 files.

**5. Ellipsis-as-stage-direction** (41 nodes). `"...Sit down. Please."` / `"...Oh. OH."` / `"…Can you look?"` Cheap staging of a realization beat. Same trick, same position in the line, everywhere.

**6. "the whole of / the entire" as closer** (12+): "that's the whole of your qualification", "That's the whole of the etiquette", "That is the entire business", "the entire extent of my involvement", "the whole of what membership consists of."

**7. Explicit triads** (11 labelled + many unlabelled). Some is quest design (3 solution paths), but the *prose* mirrors it: "Grip, wind, and whether you quit before I do — three measures." · "chair, fire, food, in that order" · "intent, shape, and cost" · "Sewers, scavengers, paperwork. Pick one."

**8. Same joke reused across files.** The recursive-bureaucracy gag runs three times in three voices:
- `pallass_den_keeper.json` — "My tin is in a queue. I am in the queue behind my tin."
- `pallass_lift_attendant.json` — "the office cannot clear the queue until the queue clears the office. Nobody may skip it. That includes the queue."
- `pallass_den_keeper.json` again — "Countersigned. By the office that was waiting on the queue, about the queue."

Model found a groove and stayed in it.

**9. Every reveal is the same reveal.** Four quests, one reframe — *the apparent object isn't the real one*:
- bushel shortfall = shame, not theft (`riverfarm_tallyman.json`)
- nine failed blades = recovery, not hardness (`pallass_forge_smith.json`)
- heirloom = the setting, not the stone (`invrisil_stationer_client.json`)
- the ward = keeping, not imprisoning (`pisces_seal.json`)

**10. No one is boring.** No rambling, no non-sequitur, no unfunny NPC, no dropped thread, no ugly line, no typo. Illiterate villagers speak in balanced parallel clauses. Uniform literacy across social class is the strongest structural tell in the corpus.

**11. Combat barks are one template, five files.** Sensory sentence + dry understatement closer:
> "It does not hurry. Things armored like that rarely need to." (`crab_nest.json`) · "It has not decided you are a threat yet." (`corusdeer_range.json`) · "Alone, they'd already be running. There are two of them." (`razorbeak_nest.json`) · "It hasn't been told you're not a shipment." (`forge_calibration_golem.json`)

## Cheapest fixes if you want it to read human

1. **Kill 70% of buttons.** Let nodes end mid-thought or on a flat fact.
2. **Cap antithesis at one per NPC, total.**
3. **Delete every CAPS word and every mid-line `...`.** Both are typography, not voice.
4. **Break register by class.** Someone should repeat themselves, lose the thread, or be genuinely dull.
5. **Stop letting characters name the theme.** "Nobody was wrong. That was the whole problem." should be the player's realization, not the smith's line.
