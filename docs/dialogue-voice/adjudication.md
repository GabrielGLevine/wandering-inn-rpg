## The exhibit rule (new, binding)

A line quoted verbatim in the critique may survive only if **(a)** the critique itself credits it (rags `served` — "best-written line in the corpus"), or **(b)** its register defends it independent of the pattern (hunter's "Fences before deer." — a T1 field maxim that predates the template it resembles). A line quoted *as damage*, on a speaker archetype the critique names, survives on neither ground. "Funding, not evidence." and the lift-queue sentence fail the test. "Tell no one.", "Habit, not you.", "Fences before deer." pass it and stand.
