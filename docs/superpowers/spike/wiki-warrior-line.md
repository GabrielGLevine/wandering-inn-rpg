# The Wandering Inn Wiki — Warrior-Line Classes & Skills

Research spike for the warrior class tree, notable warrior-line Skills, tank/shield
flavor, and Relc Grasstongue's specific classes/skills. Source of truth:
`thewanderinginn.fandom.com`. Note: the Fandom domain returned HTTP 402/403 to
automated fetches during this research pass; content was retrieved instead from its
live, same-content mirror `wiki.wanderinginn.com` (cross-linked from Fandom search
results, same MediaWiki export) plus a handful of direct Fandom page fetches that did
succeed. All URLs are cited per claim in `## Sources`.

**Read this before using anything below for game copy:** the wiki itself leaves many
skill *effects* undocumented (name + chapter cite only, no description). Those are
explicitly marked "name only, no effect text" — do not invent flavor text for them.

## 1. The warrior-family class tree

### Base/root classes

**[Warrior]** — the default combat class; base of the whole tree. No formal "how
earned" ritual is documented; implied default for anyone who takes up fighting.
The Classes page's general capstone-mechanic note cites "[Warriors] learning a new
form of blades or challenging a great foe" as exactly the kind of "small change of
perspective" that unlocks early capstone advancement (early levels are easy to
re-class off of; post-Level-50 capstones need "a life-changing experience").
Holders: Yvlon Byres (Lv.22, pre-injury), Calruz, Ksmvr (early), Lyonette du
Marquin, Rags, Selys Shivertail, ~60 characters total in Category:Warriors.
Specializes into: [Spearmaster], [Swordsman]/[Swordswoman], [Swordslayer],
[Knight], [Vanguard], [General], [Bannerman]/[Bannerlady], [Berserker], [Wounded
Warrior] (circumstantial, permanent disability), [Veteran Warrior] →
[Exemplar Warrior]. Consolidates with other classes: [Warrior]+[Strategist] =
[Commander]; [Warrior]+[Rider] = [Dragoon].
*(Classes; List of Classes/W; Yvlon Byres)*

**[Fighter]** — explicitly a **civilian, alternate track to [Warrior]**, not a
sub-tier of it ("civilian class, alternate to [Warriors]"). Introduced narratively
in "The Antinium Wars (Pt. 2)." Holders: Calruz, Gregor, Migni, Pladiune.
Specializes into Axe Fighters, Fist Fighters (Alber, Geram Redfist), Knife
Fighters. [Sword Dancer] is flagged by the wiki itself as ambiguous — "either
basic or specialized [Fighter]. Possibly synonymous to [Blade Dancer]."
*(List of Classes/F; Category:Fighters)*

**[Soldier]** — "combat class; details unknown; professional military of all
races." Granted to enlisted military personnel. Holders: Relc Grasstongue
(formerly, pre-Watch), Twin Stripes, Selys Shivertail, Touma the Great, Osthia
Blackwing, Vess (21 pages in Category:Soldiers, including [Corporal] and
[Sergeant] sub-ranks). [Footsoldier] is an "advanced or alternate [Soldier]."
Soldiers transition out into e.g. [Guardsman] (Relc "retired" from the army into
the Watch) or up into a weapon-master class (Relc gained [Spearmaster] by killing
an enemy [Spearmaster] in battle while still a soldier).
*(List of Classes/S; Category:Soldiers; Relc Grasstongue)*

**[Guardsman]/[Guardswoman]/[Guardspeople]** — city-watch/guard-duty combat
class; no wiki mechanical description beyond character examples. Earned by
joining a city's Guard/Watch (Klbkch "started to work for the City Watch as a
[Guardsman]"). Holders: Relc Grasstongue, Klbkch, Beilmark. Specializes into
**[Guardsman of Trust]** ("Senior Guardsman" rank — required ~400 citizen votes
for Klbkch), held by both Relc and Klbkch. For Relc, [Guardsman of Trust] later
consolidated with [Sergeant] into **[Trusted Sergeant of the Watch]**.
*(List of Classes/G; Klbkch; Relc Grasstongue)*

### Weapon-specialization classes

**[Swordsman]/[Swordswoman]** — "combat class, melee, sword," specialized
[Warrior]. Holders: Crusader 51 (also [Templar]), Zeter, Shellc, Calac Crusland,
and Ylawes Byres (whose [Knight] is derived from [Swordsman]). Advances into
**[Swordmaster]** (Level-30 requirement, obtainable via certification or by
defeating another [Swordmaster]) → **[Sword Saint]/[Sword Sage]** (both flagged
by the wiki as "advanced [Swordmaster]?" — uncertain exact lineage). Ylawes's
path: [Swordsman] → [Knight] → [Knight-Seeker of the Silver Dragon].
*(List of Classes/S; Ylawes Byres; Category:Swordsmen)*

**[Sword Dancer]** — "combat class, melee, agility"; the wiki flags its lineage
as unknown, "either basic or specialized [Fighter]," possibly synonymous with
[Blade Dancer] (held by Pekona). Only confirmed holder: Toren.
*(List of Classes/S; Category:Sword Dancers; List of Classes/B)*

**[Spearmaster]/[Spearmistress]** — "combat class, melee, spear," Level-30
requirement, "obtained through certification or defeat of another [Spearmaster]."
Relc's documented path is the clearest in-fiction example: military
[Sergeant]/[Soldier] → **[Spearmaster]** (by killing an enemy [Spearmaster] in
battle) → **[Vanguard of the Spear]** → **[Spearmaster]** again (re-attained,
higher) → **[Spearmaster: The Spear of Silence]** at Lv.40 (a named/prestige
variant). **[Peerless Spearmaster]** exists as a further evolution "after
reaching a certain amount of renown or respect in their class." **[Spearmaster
of the Wolf]** (Lulv Manushir) is another specialized variant. Other holders:
Tekshia Shivertail, Sserys, Rells, Xil.
*(List of Classes/S; Relc Grasstongue; Classes; Category:Spearmasters)*

**[Blademaster]/[Blademistress]** — combat class, no description text in the
class-list table beyond category. Holder: Orthenon. Grouped with the
sword/blade family alongside [Swordmaster]/[Sword Saint], but no explicit
[Warrior]-lineage statement was found for it — flag as thinner-sourced.
*(List of Classes/B)*

**[Swordslayer]/[Sword Slayer]** — "combat class, melee, sword," specialized
[Warrior] (or [Swordsman]). Holder: Klbkch, whose levels in it dropped multiple
times across several deaths/resurrections (each death costs ~10 levels,
typically off the highest class) — Level 64 pre-Rhir-rebirth, Level 44 +
[Assassin] Lv.26 earlier in life, Level 22 post-rebirth alongside [Guardsman]
Lv.13.
*(List of Classes/S; Klbkch)*

**Axe-related classes** — genuinely thin on the wiki. **[Axe Warrior]** exists
as a Category:Axe Warriors subcategory of Category:Warriors but has only one
listed page (Daly Sullivan) and no retrievable description text. Axe Fighters
are a separate subcategory of Category:Fighters (4 pages) — a parallel, not the
same branch. A companion research pass (weapon-choice angle) additionally found
a full ladder referenced on the class-list pages: **[Axe Fighter] → [Axe
Warrior]/[Axeman]/[Axewoman] → [Axe Champion]/[Axemaster]/[Axemistress]**,
analogous to the sword/spear ladders, though per-class description text is
sparse.
*(Category:Axe Warriors; Category:Fighters; List of Classes/A-L)*

**[Berserker]** — combat class, "derived from [Warrior]" (confirmed via
Headscratcher's page: "[Berserker] Lv.20, derived from [Warrior]"). Holders:
Headscratcher (Redfang Elite Warriors, wields an enchanted golden axe — but the
class itself isn't formally tagged "axe" in wiki text), Berr (Gnoll, Lv.50+),
Dorekh, Solen. Note: **[Berserker's Rage]** is a distinct *Skill* usable outside
the [Berserker] class (Yvlon Byres used it while a [Gladiator]) — don't conflate
skill and class.
*(Headscratcher; Category:Berserkers; Yvlon Byres; Berr)*

### Elite/high-tier forms

**[Knight]** — "[Knights] are people who have the [Warrior] class of a
[Knight]"; an enormous family (60+ named variants: [Paladin], [Squire]/[Page] as
precursors, [Crusader], [Chevalier], [Cataphract], [Lancer], [Templar],
[Knight-General] (hybrid with [General]), [Knight-King], and regional/Order
variants like [Summer Knight]/[Winter Knight], [Goblin Knight], [Gura Knight],
[First Warrior] (Chandrar's equivalent)). Multiple documented earn-paths: being
a sufficiently leveled [Warrior]/[Rider]; heavy-armor/mounted combat; adherence
to a code of conduct; induction by an existing Order; royal appointment
(sometimes bypassing level requirements entirely — Durene, a low-level [Farmer],
was appointed [Paladin] directly); formal knighthood ceremony. Holders: Ylawes
Byres ([Knight] → [Knight-Seeker of the Silver Dragon], derived from
[Swordsman]), Rabbiteater ([Knight of the Dawn] → later folded into a combined
Goblin Lord/Knight/Hero title), Durene Faerise ([Paladin]/[Paladin of Fire's
Oath]), Brunkr Silverfang (a Drake exception — Drakes/Gnolls are customarily,
not categorically, excluded from Knighthood), Toren ([Skeleton Knight]).
*(Knight; Knights; Ylawes Byres; Category:Knights)*

**[Commander]/[General]** — command-tier capstones. [Commander] is explicitly
"a lesser class than [General]." [Commander] can form by consolidation:
[Warrior]+[Strategist] = [Commander]; [Commander]+[Strategist] further
consolidates into [Eleleu Strategos]. [General] holders: Sserys, Zel Shivertail
([General of the Line]), Thelican ([Great General]); specialized variants
include [General of Bows], [General of Defense], [Fort General], [Garrison
General]. A separate [Tactician]-line path also converges on [Leader]/[General].
*(List of Classes/C, /G; Classes)*

**Circumstantial/injury-driven evolutions** — **[Wounded Warrior]** ("cursed/
combat class, specialized [Warrior]") is granted after permanent disability and
comes bundled with the [Ignore Pain] skill; Yvlon Byres's path is [Warrior]
(Lv.22) → [Wounded Warrior] (after losing use of both arms to a Fire Elemental)
→ **[Silversteel Armsmistress]** (Lv.47, after losing an arm to an Adult Creler;
her arms became living silver-and-steel). **[Veteran Warrior] → [Exemplar
Warrior]** is a separate advancement chain paired with [Survivor] on the
Classes page's "Known Changes" list; The Crimson Soldier (Antinium) holds
[Exemplar Warrior] Lv.17, "advanced from [Veteran Warrior]."
*(List of Classes/W; Yvlon Byres; Classes; The Crimson Soldier)*

## 2. Warrior-line Skills

The wiki's alphabetical "Skills Effect" glossary gives verbatim effect text for
many skills, but formal *per-class* skill-grant tables for [Warrior]/[Fighter]/
[Berserker]/[Guard] were not found — the "List of Classes" pages describe
branching philosophy, not itemized grants. Per the Classes page itself: **"Not
everybody with the same class gets the same Skills. It depends on what the
person needs and desires for the system to decide which Skills to hand out."**
So the lists below are drawn from character pages and the Skills Effect glossary,
not a canonical fixed "kit" per class.

### Confirmed skills that do NOT exist under commonly-guessed names

No skill named bare **[Bash]**, bare **[Charge]**, **[Weapon Sense]**, **[Iron
Guard]**, **[Battle Fury]**, or bare **[Berserk]** was found anywhere on the
wiki. These were reasonable guesses but are not canon skill names — don't use
them as-is in game text. Closest real analogues are listed below.

### Basic tier

- **[Power Strike]** — "A weaponless attack that is stronger than normal."
  Advances into **[Minotaur Punch]**. Holders: Erin Solstice (unlocked sparring
  with Toren; later upgraded fighting alongside Calruz), Toren, Pyrite, Mars,
  Joseph Ortega. *(Skills_Effect/P; Skills)*
- **[Speed Slash]** — "Increases cutting speed." Also documented as combinable:
  four Warriors together can trigger a joint combat skill using it. *(Skills_Effect/S)*
- **[Block Charge]** — "Completely absorbs impact against shield." (closest
  canon analogue to a generic "[Charge]"/"[Bash]" skill.) *(Skills_Effect/B)*
- **[I Am Unmovable]** — "Makes the User unmovable by vastly [increasing] their
  defence to the point that they can brace a heavy assault charge, and not be
  uprooted from the spot." *(Skills_Effect/I)*
- **[Parry Blade]** — "Defensive Skill that knock[s] down an incoming blow
  once." Variant **[Parry Blade — Sevenfold Reserve]** does the same seven
  times before running out. **[Perfect Parry]**/**[Flawless Parry]** — "Lets
  the User [parry] something to the best of their ability." **[Deflecting
  Parry]** — "A Defensive Skill that deflect[s] aside an incoming attack."
  Holders of named parry variants: Rabbiteater ([Giant's Parry]), Numbtongue
  ([Graceful Parry]). *(Skills_Effect/P, /F, /I; Rabbiteater; Numbtongue)*
- **[Iron Skin]/[Ironhide]** — "A Defensive Skill for Users that have a skinned
  body, like Humans, which renders their skin much stronger and more resistant
  to harm." Race-parallel versions: **[Iron Scales]/[Ironscales]** (scaled
  races — Drakes), **[Ironshell]** (Antinium, shelled). **[Steel Skin]** —
  "makes the User's skin as hard as steel." **[Stoneskin]** — "Literally makes
  the User's skin as hard as a rock." **[Iron Will]** — "Renders User immune to
  most if not all fear effects." Relc Grasstongue holds [Thick Scales] →
  [Iron Scales] (from Ch. 1.14). *(Skills_Effect/I, /S; Relc Grasstongue)*
- **[Second Wind]** — "Restores the user's stamina/energy." Holders: Joseph
  Ortega, Rabbiteater (recovery during battles). *(Skills_Effect/S; Rabbiteater)*
- **[Dangersense]/[Danger Sense]** — "Alerts the user when they are in
  immediate danger." Holders: Halrac Everam, Ceria Springwalker, Rabbiteater,
  Relc Grasstongue. *(Skills_Effect/D; multiple character pages)*

### Mid/higher tier

- **[Minotaur Punch]** — advanced evolution of [Power Strike]; held by Erin
  Solstice. *(Skills page)*
- **[Cleaving Arc]** — "An attack that causes the User's weapon to grow larger
  and gleam while gathering the power to bisect enemies." *(Skills_Effect/C)*
- **[Devastating Slash]** — "Changes the User's weapon trajectory mid-swing
  toward an unguarded area." *(Skills_Effect/D)*
- **[Flawless Strike]** — "Pushes the User beyond their limits to make a
  perfect strike against an opponent." Defensive counterpart **[Flawless
  Defence]** — same, for "a perfect block or deflection... regardless of the
  power behind it." *(Skills_Effect/F)*
- **[Walk of the Blademaster, Path of Legends]** — very high tier: "While
  walking, in a heartbeat the User simultaneously activates multiple mastered
  blade Skills all at once and independently for each opponent." *(Skills_Effect/W)*
- **[I Fought Against Five Alone]** — "For a moment 4 other perfect copies of
  the User appear to fight beside the User." *(Skills_Effect/I)*
- **[Champion's Gear]** — "Currently equipped weapons and armor fix and tailor
  themselves... become lighter, sharper, more durable." Held by Rabbiteater.
  **[Call of the Champion]** — "Calls and challenges any [Champions] in the
  opposing army. If no one answers the call, the Skill will demoralize the
  entire army." Army/mass-combat tier. *(Skills_Effect/C; Rabbiteater)*
- **[Banked Fury]** — "Store all the rage and hatred of the User so that they
  can unleash all of it to greatly empower them." Held by Berr ([Berserker]
  Lv.50+), alongside undocumented [Catch Yourself]/[Drain Fury]/[Sundering
  Blow]. **[Berserker's Rage]** — held by Yvlon Byres: "black out and rampage
  during combat" until managed with training. *(Berr; Yvlon Byres)*

### Notable named-character kits (selected)

- **Yvlon Byres** — [Silversteel Armsmistress] Lv.47 (from [Warrior] →
  [Wounded Warrior]). Signature skill **[Armform]** — her prosthetic silversteel
  limbs "shaped into armor, bludgeons, razors, or their default skin-like
  form," with sub-forms [Armform: Duelist]/[Liquid]/[Razorkind]/[Telescoping
  Flesh]. Also [Ignore Pain], [Body: Aspect of Iron]/[Aspect of Silver], [Ox's

  of the Moon] (from [Crescent Cut]). Former equipment (pre-injury): "silver

  Byres family's canonical sword-and-shield style even though her current class
  isn't a shield class per se. *(Yvlon Byres)*
- **Ksmvr** — [Brave Skirmisher] Lv.39 (from [Warrior] → [Skirmisher]). Named
  combat skills (effects mostly undocumented on his page): [Knuckles of Iron],
  [Piercing Strikes], [Quick Movement], [Quick Slash], [Evasive Flip],
  [Masterful Ambush], [Threefold Shot], [Silver Illusion: Whirlwind of Blades],
  [Skill School – Silver Illusion (Sword School)]. *(Ksmvr)*
- **Calruz** — [Fighter]. Skills: [Gore Splash], [Hammer Blow], [Thick Skin] →
  [Steelbound Hide], [Thunder Hammer's Blow], [Whirlwind Cleave], plus a
  self-created skill [Death Before Dishonor]. *(Calruz)*
- **Rabbiteater** — [Goblin Lord {Unaffiliated} – "Rabbiteater of Champions"]
  Lv.40; [Knight of the Dawn] line. Skills include [Second Wind], [Shield Ram],
  [Grand Slash], [Giant's Parry], [Aura of the Brave], [Valor of Champions],
  [Supreme Aspect of the Champion], [Champion's Gear], [Dangersense].
  *(Rabbiteater)*
- **Numbtongue** — [Sybarite Soulbard] Lv.43 (bard/combat hybrid, from [Bard] →
  [Goblin Soulbard]). Combat-adjacent: [Expert Weapon Proficiency: Sword],
  [Flash Cut], [Graceful Parry], bardic-combat hybrids like [Ballad of Bravery],
  [Counter Melody]. *(Numbtongue)*
- **Halrac Everam** — [Knight-Archer of Lost Flames] Lv.36 (Scout/Archer line,
  not Warrior-line, but relevant analogue for a mixed combat class); skills
  include [Dangersense], [Double Shot], [Pinpoint Shot], [Piercing Shot].
  *(Halrac Everam)*
- **Ceria Springwalker** — despite a warrior-adjacent adventuring history
  (Horns of Hammerad captain), her documented classes/skills are entirely
  mage-line ([Relicbound Arctic Cryomancer], [Trickster of Crowns]) — the wiki
  does not document any surviving warrior/swordfighting skills for her; she
  pivoted classes early in the story. *(Ceria Springwalker)*

## 3. Defensive/tank flavor: [Shield] classes and guard Skills

**Top-line finding:** there is no single canonical flagship "tank class" the
way [Paladin] or [Knight] exist. Shield/guard identity is scattered across many
narrow, individually-attested classes that the System hands out contextually
based on how a character actually fights, split across two separate lineages.

### Lineage A — Warrior-line shield classes ("specialized [Warrior]")

| Class | Holder(s) | How earned |
|---|---|---|
| [Shield Captain] | Walt; Tkrn | "Indication of using a shield or being defensively oriented" (Walt); "Earned defending others with a shield" (Tkrn) |
| [Shield Defender] | — | same "shield/defensive" pattern |
| [Shield Destroyer] | — | same |
| [Shield Warrior] | Shelda | same |
| [Shieldbearer] | — | Specialized [Warrior] |

| [Shieldmaster] | — | "Around level 30" |
| [Shieldbreaker Axeman] | — | Specialized [Axeman], usually ~Lv.30+ |
| [Shieldbearer Runner] | Herove Canidus | Specialized [Runner] |

*(List of Classes/S, /B, /V)*

### Lineage B — Guard/civic-duty shield classes ("specialized [Guard]")

- **[Shieldguard]** — explicitly "Specialized [Guard]" — bridges the two
  lineages but grows out of Guard, not Warrior.
- **[Guard Captain]/[Guard Sergeant]** — "higher ranked [Guard]" (Kel).
- **[Protector]** — held by Mirn, "specialized civilian fighting class."
- **[Guardian]** (base) has no standalone description; specializes into
  [Relic Guardian] (Toren, "requires possession of Relics"), [Psychic Guardian]
  (Ressk, [Telepath]-[Guardian] hybrid), [Portal Guardian] (Liska Silverfang,
  "advanced from [Doorman]").
- **[Royal Protector]/[Royal Bodyguard]** — "enhanced class, specially
  appointed by a [King] or their family members" — patronage-granted, not
  earned via combat behavior.

*(List of Classes/G, /R, /P, /K)*

Note on **[Wall Lord]**: flagged in the task as possibly tank-related (Ilvriss's
guards) — confirmed this is **not** a combat/shield class. [Wall Lord]/[Wall
Lady] is a **noble/ruler title**, "specialized [Lord], related to Walled
Cities," held by Ilvriss and others. Not relevant to the tank archetype.

### Guard/block/taunt/damage-reduction Skills

- **[Perfect Guard]** — "Lets the User perform defensive stands to block
  attacks to the best of their ability."
- **[Shield: Great Barrier]** (Steel) — "A defensive Skill used together with
  any shield the User picks up, to form a thin, weightless translucent field
  around the shield... The barrier would slowly break down the more it is hit."
- **[Shield of the Fortress]** — "Conjures from their own shield a pale,
  sage-green shield, as wide as a street, capable of bouncing off spells and
  heavy hits."
- **[Shield of Light]** — "The User's shield projects a bigger glowing shield
  to protect."
- **[Shield of Valor]** — "Enlarges the User's shield."
- **[Spiritguard Deflection]** — "A Defensive Skill that allows the User to
  automatically react to an incoming attack by deflecting it with more power
  back, or just blocking it."
- **[Grace of the Bastille]** — "A Defensive Skill that can affect an entire
  army to defend with unnatural skill for 1 minute."
- **[Ironscales]/[Ironshell]/[Ironhide]** — species-specific hardened-body
  Defensive Skills (Drakes/Antinium/Humans respectively).

Several named skills exist only as name + chapter citation with **no
description text on the wiki** — do not invent effects for these: [Guards Up],
[Shield Wall], [Shieldwall Formation], [Bulwark Shield], [Fortified Shields],
[Shieldtaunt], [Taunt] (no description or chapter ref at all), [Taunt the Foe],
[Taunting Warcry], [Ironguard Formation]. No skill named exactly **[Iron Wall]**
was found — [Ironguard Formation] and the species [Iron-] skills are the
closest matches.

*(Skills; Skills_Effect/S, /B, /G, /T, /I, /F, /A, /P)*

### Confirmed/ruled-out characters

- **Yvlon Byres** — sword-and-shield style historically (Byres family
  specialty) but current class [Silversteel Armsmistress] is a unique
  metal-limb transformation class, not a shield class.
- **Zevara Sunderscale** — Liscor's Watch Captain, holds [Watch Captain] and
  [Guardswoman] *simultaneously* (deliberately kept the latter "to hold herself
  to a higher standard"). No shield-specific class.
- **Bearig** and **"Gerald"** were checked as possible tank-flavor leads per
  task hints — **neither holds a documented shield/tank class**. Bearig is a
  [Cook]/former [Slave]. No canon "Gerald" character with a confirmed
  shield/tank class was found — treat as an unverified lead, don't build on it.

## 4. Relc Grasstongue's classes and Skills

**Race/role:** Drake, Senior Guardsman of the Liscor City Watch under Watch
Captain Zevara Sunderscale; formerly a soldier in the Liscorian Army's 1st Wing.
Father of Embria Grasstongue. Longtime patrol partner and close friend of
Klbkch (peer relationship, not superior/subordinate).

**Classes — confirmed via direct in-text quote, not inference:**
> "I—[am] a [Trusted Sergeant of the Watch], Relc Grasstongue, a [Spearmaster]."

He holds **two classes concurrently**, matching TWI's dual-class system:

- **[Spearmaster]** (combat class) — held since at least Ch. 1.07, originally
  earned pre-story by killing an enemy [Spearmaster] in battle during his army
  service. Leveled Lv.33 (Ch.1.38/5.13) → Lv.36 (Ch.7.28) → evolved into
  **[Spearmaster: The Spear of Silence]** at Lv.40 (Halfseekers Pt. 8
  Interlude). An earlier military-era chain of [Spear Drake]/[Vanguard of the
  Spear] as ancestor classes was reported by one research pass but could only
  be sourced from AI-summarized fetches, not a direct quote — **flag as
  lower-confidence, unverified**.
- **[Guardsman]** → **[Guardsman of Trust]** → **[Trusted Sergeant of the
  Watch]** (Watch/command class) — Ch.1.07 (Lv.12) → Lv.17 (Ch.7.28) →
  [Guardsman of Trust] Lv.20 ("Senior Guardsman Relc" Interlude) → Lv.24
  (Ch.8.34 R) → consolidated with his separate [Sergeant] class into [Trusted
  Sergeant of the Watch] at Ch.8.34 R, later cited at Lv.26.
- Minor/background classes: [Archer], [Carpenter] (negligible levels).

**Skills (from the Leveling History page, chapter-tagged where known):**
[Thick Scales] → [Iron Scales] (Ch.1.14); [Triple Thrust], [Dangersense]
(Ch.1.38); [Indomitable Will] (Ch.1.42); [Lightning Sprint] (Ch.4.30);
[Pierce Thrust], [Whirlwind Dodge], [Spear Dance: The Fish Leap], [Flurry
Strikes] ("The Gecko of Illusions"); [Recall Weapon: Spear], [Eyes In The
Back] (Ch.7.28); [Wear My Integrity] ("Senior Guardsman Relc" Interlude);
a large batch tied to his [Trusted Sergeant of the Watch] class-up at Ch.8.34
R: [Spear Flurry], [Consecutive Spear Flurry], [Extended Sweep], [Armored
Advance], [Counter Backstep], [Sergeant's Command], [Officer Headhunter Mode],
[The Law Upheld], [Squad: Incorruptible Watchmen], [Squad: Unity's
Coordination].

A further set of skill names surfaced only in AI-summarized page reads
(medium confidence, names plausible but not independently pinned to a chapter
via direct quote): [Spear Dance: Catch the Lightning], [Magebane Armaments],
[Summon Temporary Weapon: Spear (Mundane)], [Cut of Mirrors],
[Defensive Whirl], [Detect Guilt].

**Weapon/identity:** Relc's signature weapon is an eight-foot enchanted
anti-magic spear awarded for valor during his army service, reportedly
effective at piercing magical barriers — central to essentially all of his
named combat skills (the [Spear Flurry]/[Spear Dance] family, [Recall Weapon:
Spear], [Pierce Thrust], [Triple Thrust]). Quote: "I get lonely without a
spear. It's my security spear! I need it to sleep!" He left the spear behind
when leaving the army; his daughter Embria later returned it to him.

**Notable feats:** killed his first Wyvern in his mid-to-late 20s (sources
disagree 26 vs 28 — unresolved, low-confidence on exact number); killed Garen

## 5. How weapon choice drives class evolution

This is a **directly and explicitly documented mechanic** on the wiki's
Classes page, not fan inference:

> "It is possible for classes to improve and receive a different name later on.
> One reason for a change could be due to a specialization in a certain
> field... A [Warrior], for instance, might decide to solely focus on using a
> specific weapon, like a spear, which might result in them becoming a
> [Spearmaster] after reaching a high enough level."

This is the canonical statement of exactly the mechanic the task asked about:
**[Warrior] + sustained weapon focus + sufficient level → weapon-master
class** (sword → [Swordsman]/[Swordmaster], spear → [Spearmaster], axe → the
Axe Fighter/Warrior/Champion ladder, bow → the Archer ladder).

**General class/skill acquisition mechanics** (from the Classes and Skills
pages): classes reflect "a person's profession or vocation" and determine
Skill type; **"not everybody with the same class gets the same Skills — it
depends on what the person needs and desires for the system to decide which
Skills to hand out."** Skills "reflect a person's passions" (example given:
Pisces only has bone-related skills because he's fascinated by and studies
bones specifically). A load-bearing in-text worked example: Erin Solstice does
**not** level in [Tactician] despite objectively skilled chess play, because
she perceives it "just as a game" rather than a serious pursuit — i.e.
self-perception/intent gates class acquisition, not raw competence alone. The
Skills page further distinguishes **obtained** Skills (granted automatically
on level-up) from **learned** Skills (must be personally practiced/executed at
least once, independent of leveling).

**"Off-classes":** "Classes are known to typically change in intervals of ten
levels. If a class changes in between those intervals, they are sometimes
called 'off-classes' because they are considered the results of mismanaged
synergies and desperation, and thus judged substandard by others." Relevant
nuance for game design: an abrupt weapon/style switch mid-career isn't "free"
in canon — it risks producing a socially-stigmatized, off-schedule class
rather than a clean evolution.

**Unique/unusual classes tied to unconventional actions:** confirmed as a real
category (e.g. [Kicker], a real class per the class-list page, held by Joseph
and Ekirra Silverfang) — the System can produce highly idiosyncratic classes
matching unconventional pursuits, not just standard weapon-type ladders.
Higher-level "unique classes" also appear as narrow transcendent
specializations of core classes (e.g. [Mage] → [Necromancer]/[Cryomancer]).

**Not confirmed / explicitly ruled out during this research:**
- A search-engine-suggested claim that Ksmvr's class changed from [Skirmisher]
  to [Brave Skirmisher] *because* he picked up sword skills (Silver Illusion
  Sword School) was checked directly against his wiki page and **does not
  hold up** — no causal link is documented. Don't cite this as an example.
- [Kicker] → [Football Player] as a documented evolution — [Football Player]
  could not be found on the actual class-list page; unverified.
- No pirateaba (author) word-of-god quote explaining this mechanic was
  located on the wiki — everything cited above is the wiki's narration of
  in-story text, not an author's-note aside.
- No specific named-character on-page moment of "switched weapon → class
  changed as a result" was found beyond the generic rule-statement itself —
  this remains a gap if a citable in-fiction example is needed later.
