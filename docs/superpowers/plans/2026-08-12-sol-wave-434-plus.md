## Wave-level rules

1. **Frozen stat blocks**: all balance movement is composition-only;
   shared enemy stat rows are sim-calibration instruments (#439
   doctrine). A lane that thinks it needs a stat change stops and
   reports.
2. **rng doctrine**: any itinerary/script insertion reshuffles downstream
   fights — full re-verify, never draw-surgery; expect seed re-derivation
   and say so in commits.
3. **Windows are per-climax per-spine** [0.55, 0.85] competent-at-band;
   floor-policy losses at band are the decoupling working, not defects.
4. **Known traps** (all in `wi-writing-qa-scripts`, read it): worktree
   cwd, `--import` after checkout/merge, `WI_SPINE_WRITE=1`, pool-line
   waking idiom, panel-teardown settle, `_visible_options` drops
   accomplishment-gated rows, positional pins.
5. **User gates, batched not blocking**: new orphan-pair class names
   (B1 inventory), any spine-specific wall (C1 report), #451 reopen
   trigger, band restatement questions (dilution/economy follow-ups on
   #449's comment). Log decisions taken under wave autonomy to
   CHOICE-LOG and batch the user-owned ones into ONE ask at wave
   midpoint.
6. **Merges**: lane branches, controller gates each close
   (preflight --full + the lane's own canonical list + calibration
   suite); main stays releasable between merges. HANDOFF updated at
   every lane land, per the live-doc rule.
7. **Known red**: `d3_inventory_shot` (pre-existing inventory pin drift,
   predates the balance program) — first lane to finish early sweeps it;
   it is nobody's blocker.
