## Controller design rulings (made at plan time, recorded for review)

1. **QUALIFY = composed existing machinery, zero new sim.** The civic
   verb is expressed as clerk CONVERSATIONS that bank pass counters,
   with doors gating on those counters (`door_when` unchanged). The fee/
   permit shapes ride shipped gates: `{gold, accomplishment}` compound
   (sanctioned) on clerk options, `requires: {item}` for carried permits.
   The elevator is a door pair whose `door_when` requires
   `elevator_pass_stamped` — banked at the forge-tier clerk after
   sponsorship + fee. NO door-side fee machinery.
2. **Arrival is a quest** ("never casual entry"): 'Papers for Pallass' —
   multi-stage per the quest floor. Stage 1: Selys sponsors the
   application (Guild network canon; a real cost: 10g filing fee OR a
   completed-postings record gate — both paths real). Stage 2: the Door
   attunes (`pallass_attuned` banks at a Liscor-side beat, the portals
   row appears — the #65 stone precedent for late-attuning destinations,
   confirmed supported). Stage 3: the arrival checkpoint — the market-
   tier clerk stamps entry (the first QUALIFY beat, fee 2g, the "posted
   prices" register from line one).
3. **Race-variant pool lines: the ONE new-machinery candidate.** Pallass
   is Drake-supermajority; a Human PC needs friction lines, a Drake PC
   "assumed-local" lines. Smallest shape: `text_variants`/`talk_pool`
   entries gain an optional `requires: {race: "human"}` key —
   `_meets` learns ONE read-only cosmetic key (pc_race set at
   char-creation, additive save field already shipped). Sim change is
   ~5 lines + validator arm. If review finds a zero-sim alternative of
   equal honesty, prefer it; otherwise this is sanctioned here.
4. **Palette quarantine (third city)**: forge-bronze-on-slate; NEVER
   Liscor amber or Invrisil coin-gold accents. The A-phase tileset
   verdict must include a side-by-side quarantine check vs both cities'
   screenshots, read by controller eyes.
5. **Tier bands**: Pallass v1 content is T3 (market) / T4 (forge) per
   the region-tier table — safe-city content, no over-tier ambushes;
   combat presence v1 is ONE forge-tier hazard encounter at most
   (industrial accident register, not a war beat).
6. **Grimalkin fields as the anchor** (no equivalent substitution
   without a new wiki pass): [Sinew Magus] register — drill-sergeant
   scholar, measures everything, respects effort not rank. His v1 role
   is the bureaucratic quest's examiner beat + a talk_pool presence.
   Sprite via the PixelLab v2 character pipeline from the CORRECTED
   profile.
