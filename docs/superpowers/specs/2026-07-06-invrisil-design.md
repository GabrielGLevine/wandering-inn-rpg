# 8c — Invrisil: The Brothers of Serendipitous Meetings (DESIGN)

Status: user-seeded (big city, hideouts, rogues, HUMAN enemies).
DEPENDS on the Skills wave (stealth/movement verbs are load-bearing).
Fable-authored with art/quest/dialogue direction. Canon: the Brothers =
the gentleman-rogue guild of honorable muggers; Invrisil = the City of
Adventurers. Wiki-verify; the Brothers' named members (e.g. Normen,
Alcaz) checked at content time.

## 1. Maps (3)

- **Invrisil boulevard** (~28x18): the scale-shock map — Liscor is a
  town, THIS is a city. Art card "marble and coin": pale stone, tall
  facades (2-cell building fronts), lamp rows (light anchors ×8 budget),
  crowd DENSITY via background walk-on NPCs (6-8 pool-less dressers —
  the first map where extras exist; cheap citizen variants via
  PixelLab batch).
- **The mercantile alleys** (~20x14): the stealth playground — bent
  routes, stacked crates (burnable? no — CLIMBABLE flavor), 2-3
  trigger_radius footpad encounters SNEAKABLE past (the K2 payoff),
  fence-lit night card "lantern maze".
- **The Brothers' parlor** (hideout interior ~14x10): shabby-genteel —
  mismatched fine furniture, a card table, tea service. Card "borrowed
  elegance": warm low light, one absurdly good rug.

## 2. Characters (profiles-first)

- **A Brothers lieutenant** (canon-named if verifiable, else original+
  flag): impeccable manners, menace as courtesy. Voice: "sir/miss"
  every sentence, apologizes BEFORE the threat.
- **A merchant-prince antagonist** (original+flag): the quest's true
  villain — respectable, hires thugs, launders reputation. HUMAN enemy
  register: his hired blades = the first all-human combatant family
  (art: doublets + clubs/knives — NOT Watch-alike; distinct palette).
- **A street fixer** (informant surface; observe/friendly-rich).

## 3. Quest design: "A Gentleman's Disagreement" (3-path parity)

The Brothers ask a favor: a merchant-prince framed one of theirs for a
theft HE commissioned. Clear the Brother's name.
- **STEALTH/SKILL:** infiltrate the merchant's counting-house through
  the alleys (sneak past patrol encounters — K2's showcase), lift the
  ledger ([Observe] the true books; a keen-eye hidden-safe beat).
- **TALK:** work the fixer + a rival merchant into testifying
  ([Diplomat] chain; gold grease optional — economy sink).
- **FIGHT:** force the confession — his hired blades in a warehouse
  arena (human-enemy roster debut; the lieutenant fields as a context
  ally — party machinery reuse).
- Resolution: the Brothers owe you (their marker = a fence/vendor
  surface + a safe-passage perk in the alleys [footpad encounters go
  dormant — the dormant machinery as a REWARD]); the merchant's fate
  = a 2-option epilogue (expose vs extort — a rare moral fork, both
  opaque-safe; flag for user taste).
- Arrival: Door attunement via a Brothers letter delivered to the
  Guild board (the board again seeds the expansion).

## 4. Dialogue direction (keys)

- Lieutenant: "We don't steal, sir. We RECOVER. Occasionally we
  recover things that were never lost. Tonight we'd like to recover a
  man's good name — his, and by unfortunate arithmetic, ours."
- Merchant-prince: "Do you know what reputation costs in this city?
  More than you. Considerably more than him."
- Fixer: sells rumors by the CUP — tea-house pricing bit.

## 5. Encounters/QA/scope

Footpads (sneakable, 2 variants), hired blades (3-roster warehouse
fight), a rooftop-shortcut movement-skill beat. Canonicals:
`invrisil_walkthrough` + per-path scripts. Non-goals: Magnolia
Reinhart (adjacent canon — too big); Invrisil's adventurer economy;
player membership in the Brothers (a stage-2 hook, not v1).
