# Social Pillar II — Relationship Progression (DESIGN)

Status: user-ratified direction 2026-07-06, SIMPLIFIED per user: LINEAR
progression, not an approval/points system — NPCs visibly EVOLVE
through ordered stages (the Lyonette-thaw model generalized), no
BG3-style scoring. Chain step 6; coordinator authors the task plan at
the boundary; ⚑ taste calls at plan time.

## 1. The system: linear stages per NPC

- Each arc NPC has 2-3 ORDERED STAGES (Lyonette already models it:
  base → thawed-on-resolved_wrong_order). A stage advances when its
  specific, authored condition is met — a milestone, not a meter:
  "helped with their quest", "talked across N different wakings",
  "completed the cisterns with the TALK path". Conditions are existing
  counters read as simple thresholds/flags — the talk_pool_post gate
  machinery generalized to a per-NPC ordered stage list.
- Per stage: the talk_pool swaps, 1-2 dialogue options unlock (deeper
  topics), and the FINAL stage grants one small mechanical perk in
  visible currencies (Krshia: shop -1..2g; Erin: a daily meal; Relc: a
  repeatable spar; Selys: first pick at the board; Olesm: a chess
  counter beat; Zevara: watch-bounty access) — ⚑ perk list at plan time.
- NEVER shown as numbers/bars/hearts. The NPC just changes — pool
  voice warms, new topics appear, the perk arrives diegetically.
- No decay, no points, no gift-scoring. Gifts MAY exist as one-off
  authored stage conditions where canon-apt (bring Erin a rare
  ingredient) — flavor beats, not an economy.

## 2. Scope

v1: the 7 profiled NPCs + Lyonette (retrofit her thaw INTO the stage
list shape as the reference implementation). Writing surface: ~2 pool
swaps + option unlocks per NPC, canon-voiced per profiles. QA: one
`stages_loop` canonical driving one NPC base→final + stage-derivation
units (pure, the acts precedent).

## 3. ⚑ At plan time (user)

Final perk list; the per-NPC stage conditions table skim (each should
feel like a story beat, not a chore).

## 4. Non-goals v1

Approval meters/points of any kind; romance; companion recruitment;
warmth decay; faction reputation; >3 stages per NPC.
