# Home-Region Content II — Liscor + Floodplains (DESIGN)

Status: user-directed 2026-07-06 (more quests/encounters in the home
region alongside deepening/expanding). Fable-authored with art/quest/
dialogue direction. Slots WITH or right after M-DEPTH (its interiors +
boards are giver-surfaces for this content). Wiki-verify all canon.

## 1. New encounters (the floodplains grow teeth + texture)

- **Rock Crabs** (canon floodplains icon — boulder-mimic ambushers):
  a "boulder" prop that IS an encounter (interact/adjacency reveals —
  the dormant-visual machinery inverted: disguised-until-provoked;
  observe TELLS it apart, the [Observe] pillar's best trick yet).
  Art: v2 pipeline, boulder-shell palette matched to the existing
  boulder prop (the mimicry must actually work on the player).
- **Corusdeer herd** (canon; night-gated per the Riverfarm seam once
  it lands, day-plausible v1): NON-hostile herd wandering the north
  floodplains — a peaceful-wildlife first (fight = meat+antler loot;
  observe/[Charming Smile] = watch them pass, banks flavor counters;
  attacking scatters the herd for the waking). Encounters that aren't
  ENEMIES deepen the world read.
- **A goblin raid variant escalation** (post-Act-II gated): larger
  raid roster on the gate road — the world reacting to the acts.

## 2. New quests (3, three-path parity, existing+M-DEPTH surfaces)

- **"The Antler Order"** (Runner's Guild delivery arc → quest): a
  standing delivery becomes a story — the recipient (a floodplains
  hermit-alchemist, NEW walk-on, profile first) wants Corusdeer antler
  ETHICALLY sourced. FIGHT: cull one from the herd; TALK: trade the
  hermit's remedy to Krshia for shed antlers she stocks; SKILL:
  observe the herd's shedding ground and gather at dawn.
- **"Cellar Rights"** (the Dark Cellar pays off — DP-adjacent):
  Krshia and the watch-sergeant DISPUTE the cleared cellar's use
  (storage vs evidence). Mediate ([Diplomat] showcase), muscle
  (clear the squatter-vermin that moved in), or survey ([Observe]
  the old smuggler markings that settle ownership). Resolution
  dresses the cellar per the winner — the map remembers.
- **"The Bridge Toll"** (floodplains south, new micro-area dressing):
  an enterprising goblin (non-hostile! canon-plausible outlier)
  "collects tolls" at a plank bridge. Pay (economy sink, repeatable
  gag), fight (he flees, returns after 2 sleeps — dormant machinery
  as comedy), talk (a [Diplomat] chain that ends with him "hired" as
  a lookout — banks a unique counter; his pool thereafter reports
  road gossip = a mechanical informant). The game's first joke quest;
  voice-lint hard (the humor must be SPECIFIC).

## 3. Art direction

Rock Crab (shell-match discipline), Corusdeer (proud silhouette,
ember-orange antler accents — dusk herd shots will be the prettiest
screenshots the game has; direction card note for the north field),
hermit + toll-goblin per profiles-first. No new maps — micro-area
dressing only (the shedding ground, the plank bridge).

## 4. Dialogue keys

- Hermit: "Antler freely shed carries the year in it. Antler taken
  carries the taking. I pay for the FIRST kind."
- Toll-goblin (broken-Common, dignity intact): "Bridge MINE. Toll two
  gold. …One gold? One gold and shiny rock. Deal-deal."
- Krshia (cellar dispute): "Evidence, she says. Hrr. Evidence of what
  — that a cellar is USEFUL? Silverfangs knew that a war ago."

## 5. QA/scope

Canonicals: one per quest (+paths where streams split per C3
precedent); Rock Crab reveal + herd behavior in a wildlife script.
Non-goals: Corusdeer stampede events; goblin faction system; more
than one joke quest (comedy concentration risk).
