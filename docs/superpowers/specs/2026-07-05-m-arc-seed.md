## 1. Shape: three acts over the existing world

Canon anchor: the player is a Wandering-Inn-style otherworlder arrival
(the game already opens classless and lost — lean in). The arc is THE
reason to do everything the systems already offer.

- **ACT I — Arrival (exists, needs framing):** the current onboarding
  (Relc, spar, ambush, gate district) + a new opening beat — **the GDI
  black-screen open (user directive 2026-07-06):** a new game opens on
  BLACK with the Grand Design's voice as centered text (the same
  renderer as the sleep sequence, M-JUICE §4) — a few lines of arrival
  (far from home; this world levels people), then fade in at the inn.
  Replaces the appear-in-inn cold open; title_flow re-path disclosed.
  Act gate: reach the inn + first class gained.
- **ACT II — Finding your feet (the systems act):** Erin's inn as home
  base; the pillars ARE the act — work/social/combat/exploration quests
  (Missing Crate, Content Wave's two, economy loop) now hang off a
  visible act-line in the journal ("Make a place for yourself") with 3-4
  milestone beats (earn N classes/levels opaque-safe: beat text keyed to
  accomplishments, never numbers; e.g. "You've started to become
  someone here."). Act gate: a threshold of world-engagement counters
  (design at brainstorm).
- **ACT III — The cellar under Liscor (climax):** the sewers (Content
  Wave's map) escalate: something is WRONG under the city (canon-shaped:
  a Shield Spider brood-nest, or — brainstorm call — a Raskghar/undead
  tease per early-arc Liscor canon). One climax dungeon run + the
  game's first BOSS (new arena + one boss combatant with a gimmick that
  exercises the tactical sim: line-skill denial, adds, a positioning
  mechanic — NO new sim machinery beyond a data-expressible gimmick if
  possible). Zevara/Olesm/Erin beats before/after. Ends with a real
  ENDING SCREEN (demo closure + "the story continues at
  wanderinginn.com" — the fan-work's love letter) + post-game free play.
