## Survey: what's currently a flat surface that wants a door

1. **The Adventurer's Guild** (user's example — HIGHEST value):
   `guild_door` (street 28,3) is a toast. Becomes a real interior:
   Selys RELOCATES behind the guild desk (her street placement was
   always a stand-in for this), Olesm's natural haunt, adventurer
   background NPCs, and **the request board** — a repeatable-quest
   surface (rotating small bounties re-armed like pools; feeds gold +
   counters; the natural home for future community-submitted quests).
   The board is the mechanical payoff, not just dressing.
2. **The inn upstairs**: guest rooms + YOUR room (sleeping in your own
   bed as the canonical sleep spot; a personal-space storytelling
   surface — and the future [Garden of Sanctuary] hook lives at the
   inn, so upstairs establishes the inn-as-home grammar early).
3. **Krshia's shop interior**: the stall becomes her doorway; the shop
   inside carries the full inventory + Silverfang dressing (and the
   Wrong Order smooth-over gains a place to happen).
4. **The Watch barracks**: Zevara's context — a small interior off the
   gate (briefing room feel); natural quest-giver venue for watch-
   flavored bounties; a cell room (canon Liscor jail) for future
   quest outcomes.
5. **The Dark Cellar** (street): `cellar_door` is studied in the crate
   quest but never entered — a one-room cellar interior pays off the
   mystery (and [Light]/glow gets another showcase).
6. **Market depth (non-interior)**: 2-3 more interactive stalls with
   flavor/observe/buy surfaces; the street reads as a living market.

## Mechanisms (all existing)

Door machinery + door_when gating; small-map schema; direction cards
per interior (mood pins: guild = warm bureaucratic; barracks = stern;
cellar = dark); talk_pool/observe/friendly_line on new NPCs per the
profiles contract; the request board = a light data seam (rotating
bounty pool re-armed at sleep — the talk-pool rotation idiom applied
to quests; spec at plan time).

## Scope guard

v1 = Guild interior (with the board) + inn upstairs + ONE of
(Krshia's / barracks / cellar) — the rest queue. Every interior ships
with: map + card + dressed entities + at least one mechanical surface
(board / bed / shop / cell), never empty rooms. QA: one walkthrough
canonical per interior (gate_district precedent).
