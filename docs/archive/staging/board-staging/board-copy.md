## 2. Selys — board-adjacent desk lines (DP1 relocation: behind the Guild desk)

These extend her desk-context graph. Register: dry, competent, secretly
soft-hearted — the softness shows in what she steers you toward, never in
the wording.

**Pool/desk line, board unexamined this waking:**

> "The board's behind you. Reading it is free. Taking a posting means telling
> me, so I can log it and not have to chase you across Liscor."

**Accept (the desk logs your slip):**

> "That one? Fine. Logged. Don't die over a handful of gold — it's paperwork
> for me and embarrassing for you."

**Accept — the road-cull posting specifically (the soft steer):**

> "Good pick, actually. The road's been bad and the Watch pays on time.
> Take the south path back. I didn't say that."

**Turn-in, condition met:**

> "Done? …So it is. Here — counted twice, because the last adventurer counted
> once, loudly, and was wrong. The Guild thanks you. I'm the Guild. So:
> thanks."

**Turn-in attempted, condition NOT met (opaque-safe: no progress numbers):**

> "The notice says proof. Bring the proof, not the story. The story's free
> and so is my time apparently."

**Slate rotated overnight (first desk talk after a sleep swapped the postings):**

> "New paper went up this morning. Old postings come down whether they're
> done or not — ink's cheap, wall space isn't."

## 3. The Runner's Guild counter (DP5)

### Delivery board — header line

> THE DELIVERY BOARD. Legs and ledgers. Gold by distance, paid on the mark,
> same-waking terms. Slips at the counter.

**Standing notice (static footer):**

> Couriers are made, not hired. Start with the market run. — the counter

### Street Runner clerk — counter lines

Register per the profile proposal below: young, out-of-breath-professional —
clipped bursts, everything measured in legs and distances, borrowed
professional pride.

**First approach / issue surface open:**

> "Runner's Guild! Board's live — take a slip, show me, go. Rules, quick:
> the parcel's yours until it's theirs. Sleep on a run and it comes back
> here, and you carry the shame instead. No pay for shame."

**Issue — standard slip:**

> "Signed, stamped, yours. That's a one-leg run, easy pace. Don't walk it
> TOO easy, the term's same-waking."

**Issue — the cross-map (inn hamper) slip:**

> "Gate, floodplains, the hill. Two legs and the road's got goblins, which
> is why it pays three. Runners move FAST — that's the whole trade. Fast
> things don't get grabbed."

**Issue — the fragile (phials) slip:**

> "That one's glass. GLASS. Walk like it's your grandmother's teeth. The
> crew counts the phials in front of you, so arrive with six."

**Turn-in — mark confirmed:**

> "Mark confirmed — hah — sorry, came in at a sprint myself. Coin's counted.
> Nice legs on that run. Hawk started on a board like this one, you know.
> Well. A board LIKE it."

*(Canon: Hawk — Rabbit Beastkin Courier based in Liscor, one of the fastest
on Izril; wiki-verified 2026-07-06. Name-dropped as aspiration, never staged.)*

**Turn-in attempted, parcel not delivered / wrong state:**

> "Slip says the mark, not the counter. I can't pay you for carrying it
> AROUND, that's just… walking. Go on — daylight's a budget."

**Run failed (slept on it; parcel auto-returned — first counter talk after):**

> "Parcel came back on the night ledger. Happens. Happens ONCE, usually.
> Board's still live — take another slip and run it like you mean it."
