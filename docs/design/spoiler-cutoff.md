**TWO-TIER RULE (second ruling, 2026-07-07 — later same day):**
- **New-content bar: Book 17** (*Garden of Sanctuary*, Volume 7 Part 1).
  From this ruling forward, do not INTRODUCE any element that enters the
  story after Book 17 in new content.
- **Grandfathered:** everything already shipped or already planned as of
  this ruling stays as scoped — including Wilovan/Ratici in 8c (mid-Vol-7
  intro, past Book 17). No edits required to existing/planned content.
- **Advertised cutoff stays through Volume 7** (it must cover the
  grandfathered material). The Book 17 bar is internal discipline that
  keeps the advertised surface honest and shrinking-proof.

**The game's advertised spoiler scope: through the END OF VOLUME 7 of the
web serial** (ebooks: through the Volume 7 books — Book 17 *Garden of
Sanctuary* onward through the Vol 7 set). Advertise as:

> Spoiler-safe for readers caught up through **Volume 7** of The

The game is deliberately NOT set at any single moment in the serial's
timeline — it draws characters, places, and Skills from across Volumes
1–7. The cutoff is an upper bound on what the game may reference or
imply, not a promise to use everything under it.

## Binding rules for all content work

1. **No plot points, reveals, character fates, or Skill/class names
   introduced AFTER Volume 7** may appear in any player-facing text,
   art, or mechanic — and not in public-facing planning text (issue
   titles, README, itch page) either.
2. **"[Door of Portals]" is a VOLUME 9 name** (ch. 9.21: the door is
   unmade and reborn as a Skill at [Magical Innkeeper] Lv. 48). Through
   Volume 7 canon it is just **the Magical Door / the magic door** —
   that is the only name the game and public planning may use. (Caught
   2026-07-07; milestone/issues renamed.)
3. Cutoff-sensitive facts verified 2026-07-07 (wiki):
   - [Garden of Sanctuary]: unlocked EARLY Volume 7 (Book 17 is named
     for it) — safe.
   - Wilovan/Ratici (the Brothers, 8c): first appear mid-Volume 7
     ("Interlude – A Night in the Inn") — safe by name, including the
     hats-off tell. Their Volume 8+ arcs (Oteslia, inn-guard era) are
     PAST the cutoff — do not reference.
   - Raskghar arc + Pallass door: Volume 5. Witch arc/Belavierr:
     Volume 6. Horns of Hammerad, Rags: Volume 1+. All safe.
4. **Every new canon-check adds a cutoff check**: when verifying a
   name/fact against the wiki, also verify WHEN it enters the story —
   a canon-accurate fact from Volume 8+ is still a violation. Wiki
   pages cite chapters; a chapter number ≥ 8.00 (or an ebook mapping
   past the Vol 7 set) fails the gate.
5. Where the wiki is ambiguous about first-appearance timing, treat the
   item as PAST the cutoff until proven otherwise, or re-flag to the
   user.
