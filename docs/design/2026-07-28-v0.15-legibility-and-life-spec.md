## Lane A — the narrative delivery layer

### A1. Pending-beat openings (ruling 2)
- `acts.json` beats gain an `opening` key: forward-voice copy shown
  while the beat's `when` is unmet ("Something under the city still
  needs an answer."); the existing `text` renders only once banked.
  journal.gd renders `opening` for pending beats (keeps the `· `
  marker), `text` for banked ones. Acts with no `opening` authored
  fall back to hiding the pending beat (never to outcome text).
- Copy load: every beat in acts I–V gets an opening (~20 lines,
  authored voice, spoiler-safe by construction — an opening may pose
  the question, never the answer). Closes VISUAL-LOG
  JOURNAL/ACT-IV-PENDING-ITINERARY (P3) and the playtest's
  three-lens journal-spoiler cluster.
- test_content arm: `opening` must not contain outcome markers (no
  past-tense bank verbs list — enforce the cheap greppable subset:
  the beat's own `text` must never equal its `opening`; forbid the
  words "settled", "you read", "you took" in openings — tune list at
  implementation).

### A2. Act-seam pointers
Three mainline seams land on an empty journal (warren→survey notice,
seal report→Ceria's dig, awakening→spine). Fix: a journal "Leads"
strip — a derived section listing un-started main-line hooks whose
prerequisites are met (data-driven: each hook = {requires, hide_when,
lead_text, place}). Seed exactly the three seams + the lattice
capstone return-to-Pisces lead (playtest: "no pointer back"). No new
quests; leads vanish when their quest starts. Closes the playtest's
seam cluster.

### A3. Toast delivery rework (the lossy layer)
Story/threshold toasts are destroyed by map change, dialogue open, and
combat (VISUAL-LOG UI/QUEST-START P1, TOAST/QUEUE-DROP P2; the Act V
reveal race). Fix, two halves:
- **Queue survives**: the toast queue persists across map transitions
  and dialogue (drain-after instead of clear-on; sticky semantics
  unchanged). Combat still clears (deliberate — combat has its own
  feed) but banks the queue for after.
- **Lore capture**: every toast tagged `lore: true` in data (the
  reveal quartet, arc-start pointers, threshold narrations) ALSO
  appends to the journal's Lore tab on emit — rendered or not.
  Journal Lore becomes the durable record the playtest found missing.
- Closes UI/QUEST-START (P1), TOAST/QUEUE-DROP (P2), the TALK-path
  reveal race, and the "nothing the player learns is recorded"
  finding in one mechanism.
