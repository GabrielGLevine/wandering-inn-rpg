# v0.16 — "Region Depth" (2026-07-28, Fable)

STATUS: user-directed (2026-07-28: populate non-Liscor regions with
side quests; interiors may accompany); executes AUTONOMOUSLY after
v0.15 under the never-blocked discipline — all design calls below are
controller decisions, logged here and in docs/CHOICE-LOG.md at

## Goal

Seven side quests (two per pilgrimage region + the Floodplains
slice) with full three-pillar parity, plus seven SMALL interiors
(each ≤ parlor scale)
that host quest beats and deepen exploration. Content-only — no engine
work beyond what v0.15 ships. Every quest slots the region's existing
voice and the v0.15 population surfaces (its NPCs become givers).

## Dependencies on v0.15

Lane B's new NPCs (Pallass smith + lift attendant, upgraded Invrisil
crowd) are quest givers here; the Leads strip carries these quests'
hooks; the toast/Lore layer carries their reveals. v0.16 starts after
v0.15's population PR merges (other v0.15 PRs may still be in flight —
lanes don't conflict).

## Riverfarm (#305)

### R1 — "The Flood Ledger" (village-life)
Giver: the Former Headman (his post-chain pools already carry unrest
about supplies). Hook: flood-season prep; the mill's grain ledger is
short and the village is quietly accusing itself. Routes:
- TALK: walk the ledger with the tallyman; the shortfall is a proud
  household hiding crop failure — resolve with the Headman obliquely
  (no new-lord politics on screen; the shame is the story).
  Banks `ledger_read_true`.
- HELP: labor route — help households prep for the flood ([Basic
  Cleaning]/cooking arms + carry jobs); the shortfall surfaces
  honestly through the work. Banks `flood_prep_done`.
- FIGHT: granary scavengers are real too — drive them off (reuse the
  scavenger rig family). Banks `granary_cleared`.
Terminal: `flood_ledger_settled` (any route + report). Resolution
ladder weakest-first per the shipped convention.
Interior: **the mill** (`riverfarm/mill.json`, ≤14×10) — working
interior; the ledger hearing and the HELP beats live here.

### R2 — "What the Thicket Keeps" (hunter thread)
Giver: riverfarm_hunter. Hook: the corusdeer herd won't cross the old
line in the thicket. Routes:
- TALK: read sign with the hunter, warn the village, let the herd
  re-route (the humble answer). Banks `herd_rerouted`.
- SKILL: [Detect Magic]/hedge-literacy reads a WARD SCRAP — an old
  boundary ward of the witch's earlier work, decayed weird (pays off
  detected_wardwork literacy; the witch's post-chain pool gains one
  reactive line). Banks `ward_scrap_read`.
- FIGHT: the thicket predator denning on the line (new roster-only
  combatant from an existing rig, banded per the ladder). Banks
  `thicket_cleared`.
Terminal: `thicket_answered`.
Interior: **witch hut** (`riverfarm/witch_hut.json`, small,
wardwork-dense) — the SKILL route's reading happens here; post-quest
it stays open as an observable-rich room (exploration payoff).

## Invrisil (#306)

### I1 — "A Setting for a Lady" (Hedault commission)
Giver: Hedault (home register; no seal content). Hook: a client's
heirloom needs a warded setting; Hedault will not work on it until
someone explains why the stone is wrong. Routes:
- TALK: the heirloom is paste; the client knows; the kindness route is
  brokering a setting that honors the story instead of the stone.
  Banks `heirloom_truth_kept`.
- SKILL: assist the enchant — [Appraise]/[Detect Magic] arms at the
  bench (visible-locked otherwise). Banks `setting_assisted`.
- FIGHT: the original WAS stolen years ago — recover it from a footpad
  fence (mercantile_alleys encounter, banded). Banks
  `original_recovered`.
Terminal: `setting_commissioned`.
Interior: **the stationer's** (`invrisil/stationer.json` — the ONE
b5 #220 shopfront opened fully; the client is met here; the other two
shopfronts stay observes by decision — one deep room beats three
shallow ones, logged).

### I2 — "The Hat Stays On" (parlor job)
Giver: Wilovan (parlor, `{addr}` convention throughout). Hook: a
package must change hands at a venue where nobody may raise a voice —
a gentleman's errand with etiquette constraints. Routes:
- TALK: oblique negotiation — three exchanges, never naming the trade.
  Banks `handoff_talked`.
- SKILL/SNEAK: timing-and-position navigation (plate-pattern reuse) —
  the package moves without a word. Banks `handoff_quiet`.
- FIGHT: it goes loud; the hat comes off (the canon idiom pays off ON
  the failure-turned-fight route, one fight, then the job completes
  ugly). Banks `handoff_loud`.
Terminal: `hat_job_done`. Wilovan's inn register gains one reactive
line keyed to the terminal (register-pure: manners, not business).
Interior: **the Adventurer's Rest** (`invrisil/adventurers_rest.json`,
parlor-scale common hall) — the venue; stays open post-quest as
Invrisil's ambient interior with a small talk_pool cast.

## Shared conventions (binding)

- Three-pillar parity per quest (routes are REAL: distinct fiction,
  distinct counters, resolution ladders weakest-first with
  `_resolution_order` notes — the structural guard enforces).
- Book-17 bar; regional voices per character-profiles; `{addr}` for
  any PC address; "the Magical Door" naming; no new-lord/witch-lore
  politics on screen beyond the shipped oblique framing.
- All new counters named above ARE the freeze names (first-write
  discipline); terminals feed the guest/reactive stages where named.
- Interiors: ≤ parlor scale, wi-adding-a-scene validators, arrival
  lint, no portal surfaces (walk-in only — the carrier-vs-row audit
  stays trivially green); each hosts at least one quest beat AND at
  least three non-quest observables (exploration must pay off after
  the quest closes).
- Census margin: pointers only; check per PR.
- Post-quest life: every giver gets ≥1 reactive stage keyed to their
  quest's terminal (the Riverfarm post-chain pool standard).
- DECISION LOG: every design fork taken during implementation
  (naming, route mechanics, interior layouts, cut/keep calls under
  census pressure) goes to docs/CHOICE-LOG.md — never to a user gate.
  Documented fallback: if effort or census forces cuts, interiors
  drop to one per region BEFORE any quest loses a route.
