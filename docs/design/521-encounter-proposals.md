Status: design only, 2026-10-05. Source baseline: `b1c4b02bee71ba0b0926bf1c466ad7eab65a80e6`; branch `issue/521-encounter-design`. Owned delivery: this document only. None of the proposed geometry, copy, resource examples, or outcomes below has been implemented or playtested.

The proposal preserves three existing fights and gives each a different immediate question: **which enemy deserves the next action; where should I finish this turn; whose pressure should I relieve?** It changes encounter-specific staging and communicates existing rules. It introduces no creature, Skill, region, objective system, mandatory fight, ending, companion command, or combat rule.

## 2. Current primitives and their limits

`src/core/combat/wi_combat.gd`, `combat_ai.gd`, `skill_effects.gd`, `src/core/items.gd`, and `data/skills.json` own the following rules:

- A turn starts with 4 AP and ordinarily 3 movement points; held passives/statuses can change movement. Basic attack costs 2 AP. Dash costs 1 AP and grants 3 movement. Ordinary movement consumes movement points, not AP. Use the actual fielded snapshot and effective cost, including [Quick Cast], rather than hardcoding a class's action budget.
- `[Power Strike]` (`power_strike`) costs 3 AP, requires a sword, and has a two-round cooldown. `[Second Wind]` (`second_wind`) is a self-heal of 8 for 2 AP, once per fight. A held `mending_draught` heals 8 for 1 AP; current inventory cannot carry multiple copies of the same item ID.
- `[Frost Bolt]` (`frost_bolt`) has nominal cost 1 AP / 2 MP, range 4 with line of sight, and applies `slowed` to the next movement pool. It does not cancel actions, disable a summon, or undo an already declared [Slam]. Spell costs can change with passives; MP also feeds held defensive reactions.
- The Lich immediately spends 3 AP on `[Raise Bones]`, once per round and at most twice per fight. A new thrall appears in the first free enemy spawn, joins future-round initiative, and is not a channel the player can interrupt. Killing the Lich prevents later raises but leaves living thralls to defeat. Spawn camping is not a promised denial route: every candidate must be occupied for capacity refusal, and moving thralls reopen cells.
- `[Slam]` spends 4 AP on declaration when the melee AI has an adjacent foe on an even-numbered round. It freezes a radius-one area around that target's current cell and resolves at the start of the caster's next turn. Moving out works; damaging without downing the caster does not interrupt it. Getting hit can apply `rooted`. The `windup` profile falls through to the ordinary melee arm; it is not special hazard AI.
- Wilovan acts automatically. Foes are sorted by HP; melee AI attacks an adjacent foe or paths toward the lowest-HP foe. There is no taunt, controllable bodyguard stance, interception, or ally command. Blocking a lane is only occupancy/pathing and cannot promise aggro. Ally survival is useful, not a new victory condition.
- Blocked cells affect navigation and line of sight. They are not percentage damage cover. Decorative rubble outside the board cannot confer protection.

Reachable kits: `data/classes.json` grants Warrior through Relc's spar; Warrior 1 has `power_strike`, Warrior 4 `second_wind`, Warrior 5 `dangersense`. Pisces's existing lesson grants Mage; Mage 1 has `frost_bolt`/`quick_cast`, Mage 2 adds `flame_dart` and `mana_shield`. The grant counters and subsequent sleep must be earned in a continuous proof route. No named build below is permission to inject its kit. A caster may use ordinary weapon attacks at zero MP; that establishes a legal response, not a promised win. Higher-lineage alternatives must show their inherited fielded kit and appropriate equipped weapon.

### A. Lich: spend damage on the source or buy space

**Warning and copy draft.** Keep the existing world observation's fiction; append a short mechanical encounter hint after the board appears: “The Lich raises at most two Bone Thralls. Bring it down to stop further raising; the thralls already standing remain.” At the actual arrival, preserve the current grave-colored flash, distinct thrall silhouette and feed line. This warns about priority without promising a channel-interrupt button or a guaranteed chance to prevent the first summon. Deliver this hint through the existing combat-hint presentation seam, with an encounter-specific data/config design in #522; such a mapping is presentation work, not a new battle objective.

**Rested trace.** Enter `H/H, M/M`; by a later PC turn a first thrall is in the lower lane and the Lich remains farther away. From a legal upper position, choose the Lich for an affordable range-four spell through clear line of sight, or spend movement/Dash approaching it for sword damage. The price is leaving the thrall alive to approach next round. Alternative: use a basic attack on an adjacent thrall, or [Frost Bolt] to slow a nonadjacent one, then step behind the real `[6,4]` obstruction where the computed sight line is blocked. The price is another Lich action and potentially its second raise. Source death prevents only unspent future raises; defeating a thrall does not replenish the allowance.

**Depleted trace, conditional on #571.** Enter at `ceil(0.55×H)/H, 2/M` with one previously acquired mending draught. On a turn with an adjacent thrall, choose draught (1 AP, up to +8 HP) plus ready sword [Power Strike] (3 AP), or spend the last 2 MP on a legal [Frost Bolt] and move to break the Lich's sight line. The latter trades HP recovery and potential mana-shield reserve for space; the former leaves MP for defense and spends inventory. If no potion is owned, move/attack with the existing pool or use unused [Second Wind] plus basic attack (2+2 AP). Rest before commitment remains a valid answer. An MP potion is not in the current game: section 4's future warning/cancel alternatives must work before proposing repeated casting as the answer.

**Reach and failure.** A martial-primary build (Warrior 5 is the minimum cited kit, not a claimed adequate solo balance band) and a Mage-bearing build can take the optional crypt route after `horns_dig_started`; their acquisition histories and equipment must be logged. The canonical's raised skeleton is an optional third-body variation, not the required answer. Failure should show whether the player was overwhelmed by surviving thralls or exposed to the Lich, then offer the ordinary checkpoint retry/abandon path. No extra reward per summoned death; no farming loop. Restore inventory/resources under the current checkpoint contract, then allow departure through `ruin_exit` to recovery.

### B. Kingslayer: change destination after the warning

**Warning and copy draft.** Preserve the existing pre-fight `[Back away.]` choice and apex-threat text. On a real `windup_declared`, proposed explanatory feed text: “Kingslayer Spider readies [Slam] around where its target stood. Leave that space before its next turn.” Show the current caster flash/sound to everyone. Keep exact painted cells exclusive to [Dangersense]; do not grant every build the passive for readability. For non-holders, the feed must remain readable through the decision turn and the declared target/location must be understandable from the existing visible action. If normal-speed non-holder observation cannot identify the threatened space, improve this encounter's textual location/target cue and its render evidence before accepting; do not silently universalize the overlay.

**Rested trace.** Suppose the PC is `[6,3]`, the Spider `[7,3]`, and the Spider declares on the PC. The footprint is the unblocked cells in columns 5–7, rows 2–4; `[6,2]` is excluded by `_radius_area`. On the PC's next turn, `[6,3]→[5,3]→[4,3]` costs two ordinary movement points and leaves the footprint. A sword build can use remaining movement/Dash to seek a different legal attack cell, but cannot assume a melee attack from `[4,3]`. A caster at `[4,3]` has range three to `[7,3]` and a clear horizontal sight line, so may cast an affordable spell. Staying at `[6,3]` to attack twice instead accepts the queued hit/root risk. The added `[6,2]` blocker prevents a straight north step; `[6,5]` prevents a straight south exit. Side exits still exist. The strike resolves on the old cells, not the new PC location.

**Depleted trace, conditional on #571.** Enter `ceil(0.40×H)/H, 0/M`. At that same warning state, move to `[4,3]` first; drink the owned draught for 1 AP, or spend 2 AP on unused [Second Wind]. Healing is self-targeted and does not move the PC. At zero MP, do not require a cast or mana shield: use the safe movement window to recover/reposition. If movement was already restricted, read the actual pool; a rooted PC cannot be promised that Dash provides an escape. Resolve the ordinary hit/defeat or prevent that entry by recovery/kit preparation. Compare this with a future MP-potion choice only after its HP penalty and 1-AP spend are previewed; drinking while remaining in the footprint is not counterplay.

**Reach and failure.** Use the existing `dungeon_kingslayer_loop` approach and conversation. Record an earned martial lineage with [Dangersense] and a caster or other supported build without it, each at its real available band. The existing `t4_spellsword11_party` harness label is an instrument, not proof a fresh pure Mage should win this optional apex encounter. Downing the Spider before resolution should cancel its practical threat; partial damage does not. Defeat retries from the checkpoint; backing away before combat remains free. Victory leaves the nest dormant until actual sleep, with existing bounty and loot behavior.

### C. Hired blades: relieve the ally or pressure the captain

**Warning and copy draft.** With Wilovan actually fielded: “Wilovan acts on his own. The nearer blade is closing on him; bring that attacker down or make room to reach the captain.” Only show the closing clause when the observed movement/adjacency supports it; the opening fallback is “Wilovan acts on his own. Watch who the blades can reach.” With no Wilovan, omit ally guidance. Use visible named HP/readouts and movement, not a new threat meter or a claim that enemies have locked targets.

**Rested trace.** After contact, suppose Wilovan is `[5,4]`, knife B `[5,5]`, PC `[4,4]`, and the captain remains farther east of `[6,3]/[6,4]`. PC has 4 AP. Choose two ordinary attacks on adjacent knife B, or a ready sword [Power Strike] (3 AP), to reduce its time attacking Wilovan; misses leave pressure intact. Alternatively move around the upper opening and spend AP approaching the captain, accepting that Wilovan and the knife act automatically before the next intervention. A Mage can [Frost Bolt] a still-approaching blade before contact, or deal spell damage to the adjacent threat from a legal sight line. Slowing a blade already adjacent does not reduce its attack AP, so it is not a substitute for killing it. Captain [Counter Strike] makes careless melee focus costly; it remains the existing reaction, capped by current rules.

**Depleted trace, conditional on #571.** Enter `ceil(0.45×H)/H, 2/M`; Wilovan still enters on his own current encounter-local resource rules. If the PC has lower HP than Wilovan, enemies may prioritize the PC, so the ally script must follow actual state. At the contact state, an owned draught plus [Power Strike] spends 1+3 AP, balancing self-survival with pressure relief. The competing last [Frost Bolt] spends the remaining MP and may leave no mana-shield reserve. A no-MP martial response remains move/basic attack or [Second Wind] + basic attack; it is not guaranteed to save Wilovan. Potions and [Second Wind] cannot heal him. His fall removes an allied action source but does not fail the quest, cancel victory, or change the ending.

**Reach and failure.** Talk to Wilovan through the existing `invrisil_wilovan` branch to earn `wilovan_will_fight`, then use the fight arm of `invrisil_disagreement_fight`. Repeat with the unbanked ally gate: same trio, no invented substitute ally. The solo question becomes which approaching blade to remove before confronting the captain. Test both martial and Mage-bearing earned kits; do not advertise an ally-targeted healer answer without a reachable held Skill. Retry or leave through the alley exit for recovery; do not turn testimony/ledger routes into combat requirements.
