## Picked pattern and boundary

Add a compact numeric resource strip to the **existing top launcher band**, using its safe-area calculation and established chrome. There is no current exploration resource/status strip to extend. Keep detailed preparation and potion information in inventory and the existing action-receipt surfaces. Alternative: a separate permanent resource/buff panel. Reject that as the default because it would take space from the world and duplicate detail already reachable in inventory.

The HUD-system and responsive-UI skill patterns inform screen-space placement, event-driven updates, containers, readable text and touch bounds. Repository authority remains `Game.sim`, snapshots and `ObservableBus`; do not copy a generic `HealthComponent`/second event-bus architecture. Use existing `UIChrome` theme and approved art; no new icon or panel asset is required by this contract. The art owner must settle chrome geometry before implementation.

Only this new document is owned by the preparation lane. World/UI/combat/core/event/QA/map/catalog/art files are read-only. No import, engine, browser, windowed run or shared generated output is part of its validation.

## Projection and copy contract

Read numeric state from the authoritative projection, never derive maxima in UI. Display `HP 7/24   MP 0/12`; depletion must remain visible. With `max_mp == 0`, show `MP 0/0` and explain “No MP pool” in detail. Do not call a zero-current caster a non-caster or hide its empty MP. Avoid raw attributes and progression thresholds. AP belongs only to combat.

Proposed additive view model, negotiated with #566/#568 before code:

- `vitals`: the existing five fields, unchanged in meaning.
- `preparation`: entries with displayable effect amount/type, source, `armed` or `active`, and explicit duration/expiry (`next_fight`, `this_fight`, `until_sleep`, or a settled permanent maximum). Do not invent round/time countdowns for waking/fight effects.
- `potion_safety`: doses used, safe doses remaining, next-dose HP loss, whether first-harm confirmation is required, and an explicit exposure/poison state. A dose counter alone cannot establish a continuing damage-over-time status.
- `use_preview`: item/count, allowed/refusal reason, AP cost, actual prospective HP/MP gain, HP loss, resulting resources, quantity cost and preparation changes. These values come from the same pure sim rules as commit.

A compact resource row is a state summary; receipts explain actions. Proposed example copy uses illustrative numbers, not tuning commitments:

| Settled result | Honest receipt |
|---|---|
| HP 7→15 with cap 24, advertised restore 8 | “Restored 8 HP. HP 15/24.” |
| HP 22→24 with advertised restore 8 | “Restored 2 HP. HP full: 24/24.” |
| Full pool; no restoration | “HP already full. Restored 0 HP.” State whether the item was consumed or refused according to the actual transaction. |
| Gear raises max HP 24→28, current stays 7 | “Max HP +4. HP 7/28.” Never “Healed 4 HP.” |
| Gear lowers max HP 28→24 while current was 27 | “Max HP 24. HP capped at 24/24.” This is capacity clamping, not an attack. |
| Meal both restores and arms preparation | “Restored 6 HP. Next fight: +4 max HP.” Two effects, separately itemized. |
| Equal/weaker preparation after existing strongest-per-key merge | State the unchanged armed total and “Preparation unchanged”; restoration can still occur separately. No fake stacking receipt. |
| Actual sleep | “Rested: HP 24/24 (+9), MP 12/12 (+5). MP-potion allowance reset.” Maxima are the post-progression values; any max change is a separate clause. |
| Battle consumes/ends preparation | “Preparation active — this fight”; on expiry report any max/clamp change and final carried HP/MP. |

A [`WIEffectText`](https://github.com/GabrielGLevine/wandering-inn-rpg/blob/bb5be3ed5ce4d0ca08d5d194c316bc99e48eb107/wandering_inn_game/src/core/effect_text.gd) currently labels item/meal `hp_mod` as “+N HP” and healing as combat-only. Update its shared card/receipt phrases when the mechanics land. Never change that wording ahead of the actual recovery capability. Use the sim's merged preparation result, not the item's standalone advertised bonus.

## Potion proposal and transaction boundary

The recovery plan recommends **three safe MP doses per waking, fourth onward restoring MP and immediately costing 4 HP**, plus 1 AP in combat; allowance/damage numbers remain measurement proposals. The existing HP-potion 1 AP cost is observed. Show the configured allowance and preview supplied by #568, never embed the recommended dose threshold in presentation.

Proposed detail: “MP potions: 1 safe dose left until sleep.” At the harmful boundary, show “This dose restores 5 MP, costs 4 HP” and, in combat, “1 AP”. The numbers must be actual capped restore and actual damage from the current state. Distinguish “Next dose causes mana poisoning” from “Mana poisoning: further doses cost HP”; do not imply periodic damage if only immediate per-dose loss exists. Full MP/no MP pool still needs a truthful zero-benefit preview with any applicable cost/refusal.

First harmful dose opens a warning with explicit **Cancel** and **Drink**; default selection is Cancel. Opening, back, cancel and repeated taps must spend no item, AP, exposure, HP or MP. Confirm revalidates current resources/stock/AP/risk before a single atomic commit; stale previews must refresh or refuse. Insufficient AP/stock refuses without partial effects. Recommended out-of-combat lethal refusal and normal combat defeat remain #568/#566 design work. Do not bypass defeat by clamping a harmful combat dose to 1 HP. Inventory detail, combat controls and receipt must share the pure preview/commit rules; #567 and #568 serialize edits to those shared presentation files.

## Event timing, retention and proof

Proposed settled domain payload (`player_resources_changed`, provisional name): operation ID/revision, source action/item/service/encounter, context, reason, before/after resource snapshots, restored HP/MP, HP loss, max changes/clamps, quantity/AP/exposure deltas, preparation before/after and outcome/refusal. Restoration and damage must remain separate even when net HP change is zero. Same-state no-benefit outcomes need a receipt event even when no resource-change event is warranted. Proposed `recovery_resolved`, `potion_use_offered/cancelled/refused` names require owner agreement; do not emit fake change events on cancel.

Emit after the atomic mutation is settled, before any consumer needs to save or present the completed action. Presenters capture/deep-copy the event at enqueue time, including text inputs and numeric before/after values. Proposed confirmations are `ui_resources_rendered`, `ui_recovery_rendered`, `ui_potion_warning_rendered`, carrying the operation ID, exact displayed numbers/text and surface. Emit only after layout/visibility are established. Re-showing retained feedback may emit a fresh render occurrence tied to the same operation; it must not reapply or rerecord the gameplay effect.

A [`combat_playback.capture_playback_event`](https://github.com/GabrielGLevine/wandering-inn-rpg/blob/bb5be3ed5ce4d0ca08d5d194c316bc99e48eb107/wandering_inn_game/src/combat/combat_playback.gd#L51) already deep-copies payloads and captures `_ui.stats`/feed text. Extend that discipline for future recovery events, not a dequeue-time call to live `player_resources()`. The battle may already have processed the next attack. Align the resource readout and receipt to the same played beat, and wait for playback completion before showing terminal world values. If tweened, captured integers remain the truth; cancel an obsolete tween and prove the terminal value, including reduced-motion behavior.

A [`MessageLayer` queue](https://github.com/GabrielGLevine/wandering-inn-rpg/blob/bb5be3ed5ce4d0ca08d5d194c316bc99e48eb107/wandering_inn_game/src/ui/message_layer.gd#L913) retains entries while modals block them, admits explicit `modal_response` entries, banks combat toasts and records recent messages. A [`combat_screen` toast bridge](https://github.com/GabrielGLevine/wandering-inn-rpg/blob/bb5be3ed5ce4d0ca08d5d194c316bc99e48eb107/wandering_inn_game/src/combat/combat_screen.gd#L215) mirrors authored combat copy into its feed. Preserve retained structured receipt data as well as text; a hidden receipt does not earn `ui_recovery_rendered`. Inventory use should place its action response in the inventory/allowed modal-response space; unrelated ambient messages wait. Sleep veil is layer 30 versus toast layer 12: show the settled recovery in the veil or retain it until `ui_sleep_veil_finished`. Do not let a reading timer expire behind it. Recent Messages is a reading aid (unsaved, capped), not the resource authority.

Existing ambient `toast`/`line` copy, `ui_ambience_rendered`, generic “Used”, autosave pills or “you feel rested” prose are not evidence that the actual service/action graph changed resources. The proof must traverse production input → real sim command → committed domain result → correlated visible receipt. Fixture setup may establish a precondition but cannot replace earned acquisition/service/battle/sleep triggers.
