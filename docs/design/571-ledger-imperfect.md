## Route and declared imperfect choices

The script shares `journey_rogue` (seed 9) up to Krshia's stall at 37 gold in
Act IV (step 1654, times_slept 11), so fights 1-8 below are the Rogue's own.
From there it declares three imperfect choices.

1. **Redundant spend.** It buys the Silverfang hunting knife (15) and the
   pond handline (4). The knife is never equipped beside Relc's spear and the
   rusty sword, and the route never fishes. The catalyst then takes the
   remaining 18, leaving 0 gold.
2. **Coyle exposed, not extorted.** This forgoes the 40-gold buy-off.
3. **Missed rewards.** The route never takes the optional Invrisil one-shots
   (Wilovan's hat job 25, the stationer's heirloom 25, the steward's
   portfolio 30). It sells nothing and never buys the 58-gold Krshia set that
   the Rogue history wore.

The road goblins were fought and retired in Act I, so their 2 gold is gone.
The end-of-run assertions pin every one of these absences.
